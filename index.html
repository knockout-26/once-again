What Changed For XVIII

Added to the guidelines this year. Full rulebook in the PDF.

The money is now a rule

$400 buy-in, $100 deposit by Sep 26, balance by draft night — written into the rulebook with late fees and a transaction lock for unpaid balances.

One fine schedule

Every fine, snitch split and pot contribution consolidated into a single table instead of five scattered sections.

Jabari Brown Clause extended

Points, then rebounds, then assists now breaks a 5-5-1 in the regular season too — not just the playoffs.

Commissioner conflict rule

Rulings that touch the commissioner's own team go to a panel of the three most recent champions instead.

Tanking and abandonment

With 12 of 20 making the playoffs, eliminated teams that stop setting lineups are fined, and abandoned rosters get set by the commissioner.

Appeals

Any GM can appeal within 24 hours. Six seconds sends it to a league vote.
